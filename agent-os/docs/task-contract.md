# Task contract

## Objective
{one concrete outcome}

## Constraints
{only constraints that materially affect the solution}

## Success criteria
- The requested behavior/artifact is implemented.
- Relevant verification is executed.
- Failures introduced by the change are repaired and rechecked.
- Claims that depend on external facts are grounded in appropriate sources.
- Remaining blockers, assumptions, and material risks are stated precisely.

## Autonomy
Choose the smallest useful set of tools and context. Make routine, reversible decisions within scope without stopping for approval. Stop only at a defined authority boundary or when missing information would materially change the outcome and cannot be safely inferred.

## Output
Lead with the completed result. Then report what changed, verification performed, and unresolved risks/blockers.
