---
name: research-verification
description: Verify consequential external claims with current primary or authoritative evidence.
---

Use for fact-checking, investigative research, policy/law/current-event claims, or decisions where stale facts materially change the answer.

- Convert the question into verifiable claims.
- Prefer primary sources, official records, standards, and peer-reviewed research when available.
- Use independent corroboration for disputed or high-impact claims.
- Track event date separately from publication date.
- Label fact, inference, hypothesis, and unresolved uncertainty distinctly.
- Preserve source provenance; do not cite a source for a claim it does not support.

Read `references/evidence.md` for evidence grading when the task is investigative or contested.
